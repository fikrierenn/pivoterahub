# PivoteraHub - Revenue Model & Pricing Strategy

## 💰 FREEMIUM MODEL

### FREE TIER (Deneme)
**Hedef:** Lead generation, product adoption

**Limits:**
- 5 video/ay
- Temel skorlama (hook, tempo, clarity, CTA, visual)
- Basit dashboard
- 30 günlük data retention
- Community support (email)

**Değer Önerisi:**
"İlk 5 videonuzu ücretsiz analiz edin, sistemin gücünü görün"

**Conversion Strategy:**
- Day 5: "Son 2 videonuz kaldı" email
- Video limit dolunca: "Upgrade et, sınırsız analiz" popup
- Feature teasing: "Content Calendar" (blurred) → "Upgrade to unlock"

## 📊 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY

### Anchoring
- Enterprise tier (highest) → makes Business seem reasonable
- Free tier → gets users in the door

### Decoy Effect
- Starter ($49) vs Professional ($149)
- Professional looks like much better value (3x features, 3x price)

### Tiered Value
- Each tier is 3x previous tier price
- But offers 5x more value → feels like steal

### Annual Discount
- Save 20% with annual billing
- Starter: $470/year (vs $588)
- Professional: $1,430/year (vs $1,788)
- Business: $3,830/year (vs $4,788)

## 💡 MONETIZATION STRATEGIES

### 1. Freemium Conversion Funnel
```
100 signups (Free)
  ↓ (30% convert after 30 days)
30 Starter
  ↓ (20% upgrade after 3 months)
6 Professional
  ↓ (10% upgrade after 6 months)
0.6 Business
  ↓ (5% upgrade after 12 months)
0.03 Enterprise
```

**Expected Revenue per 100 Signups:**
- Starter: 30 × $49 = $1,470/month
- Professional: 6 × $149 = $894/month
- Business: 0.6 × $399 = $239/month
- Enterprise: 0.03 × $3,000 = $90/month
- **Total: $2,693/month from 100 signups**

### 2. Usage-Based Add-Ons
- 40% of Professional users buy extra credits
- Average add-on spend: $50/month
- Professional + add-ons = $199/month effective

### 3. White-Label Resellers
- 1 reseller = 20 sub-accounts
- Reseller pays $1,000/month
- Sells to clients at $199/month
- Reseller makes $2,980/month profit

### 4. API Access (Developers)
- $299/month for 10,000 API calls
- $999/month for 100,000 API calls
- Each additional 10,000 calls: $29

## 💸 UNIT ECONOMICS

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Organic: $20/user (content marketing, SEO)
- Paid: $80/user (ads, influencer)
- **Blended CAC: $50/user**

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Starter: $49 × 12 months = $588
- Professional: $149 × 18 months = $2,682
- Business: $399 × 24 months = $9,576
- **Blended LTV: $1,500** (weighted average)

### LTV/CAC Ratio
- $1,500 / $50 = **30:1** (excellent!)

### Payback Period
- Starter: 1 month
- Professional: 0.3 months
- Business: 0.1 months

### Churn Rate (Target)
- Starter: 10%/month (high churn, low commitment)
- Professional: 5%/month
- Business: 2%/month
- Enterprise: 1%/month
